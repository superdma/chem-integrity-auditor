# d2na00842d — FeSe₂/TiO₂ supercapacitor electrode (Nanoscale Adv. 2023, 5, 1465)

- **PMC**: PMC9972855 · **notice**: PMC13591313 (Nanoscale Adv., 9 Sep 2026)
- **Run**: `gold/runs/PMC9972855`
- **Retraction wording**: “The XRD patterns and Raman data in Fig. 1, and the
  EDX data in Fig. 3 were first published in ref. 1 by different authors. The
  SEM data in Fig. 2b is partially identical to data published in ref. 1 and
  has signs of being edited.”

## Checklist

| # | Testable hypothesis | Deterministic track | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fig. 1 XRD/Raman and Fig. 3 EDX match a prior publication | cross-paper image/spectrum corpus | **not testable** — ref. 1 (Mater. Chem. Phys. 2023, 303, 127793) is not open access and is not in the run |
| 2 | Fig. 3 EDX panels were built from a common template | fingerprint + ORB within the figure | **partially reproduced** (a ↔ d shared noise, Level 2) |
| 3 | Fig. 2b SEM is internally duplicated/edited | ORB/phash within the figure | **not reproduced**; no internal duplicate |

## Evidence

**Fig. 3 (EDX, four spectra).** Panel map: 0 = (a) FeSe₂, 1 = (b) TiO₂,
2 = (c) KT-1, 3 = (d) KT-2.

- `spectral_noise_fingerprint.py`, (a) ↔ (d): `curve_r = 0.78`,
  **`noise_corr = 0.82`, verdict `shared_noise_texture`**.
- (a) ↔ (b): `noise_corr = 0.68` (shared texture); (b) ↔ (d): `0.64`.
- ORB pairs are weak (30 matches / 11 inliers for a ↔ c) because the spectra
  are mostly flat lines.
- The shared Fe/Se peak positions between (a) and (d) are expected — KT-2
  contains FeSe₂. What is not expected is the shared *background noise*
  texture between independently acquired EDX spectra. This is a Level 2 lead
  for a common template, but it does not by itself prove prior publication.

**Fig. 2 (SEM).** The whole figure is one panel after split (the images are a
tight montage). No internal ORB/phash duplicate was found among the four
sub-images, so the alleged editing is not visible at this resolution.

**Fig. 1 (XRD + Raman).** The panel splitter returns the two sub-figures, but
the traces inside each are offset stacks without separator rules, so the
current `stack_split.py` cannot isolate them. No automated per-trace
comparison was possible; visual inspection shows the three TiO₂-containing
Raman traces at nearly the same shape, which is also expected from the shared
TiO₂ phase.

## Interpretation

The notice's charges are **cross-publication** claims. The pipeline has no
prior-publication corpus, and the cited article is paywalled, so the honest
outcome is “not testable”, not “clean”. The one independent signal the
pipeline can add — shared EDX background noise inside Fig. 3 — is consistent
with the notice but is Level 2 on its own.

**Grade: Level 3 (untested cross-paper claim)** for charges 1 and 3;
**Level 2** for the EDX common-template lead.

## Benign explanations tested

- Fig. 5/6 strong ORB pairs (up to 134 matches / 98 inliers): electrochemical
  CV/GCD curves of the same electrode series; re-plots of the same shape are
  legitimate. Not graded.
- Supplement Fig. S1 segment hits (3, score 0.992): a periodic crystal-lattice
  diagram; repeated unit cells match by construction. Benign.
- `chem_numeric.py`: 1 info finding — all current densities are integers
  (1, 3, 7, 9, 12, 15 A g⁻¹), which is normal for a planned experimental
  series, not an anomaly.
