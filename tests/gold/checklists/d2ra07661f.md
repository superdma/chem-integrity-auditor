## Evidence

Fig. 1 is a two-panel figure. Panel (a) is a 2-trace XRD stack
(Al₂Cu-MOF@M-OOHv-ER/CF green, Al₂Cu-MOF/CF blue); panel (b) is the Raman
stack. `stack_split.py` returns 2 bands per panel, so the traces are compared
separately.

Measured on the green XRD band
(`stacks/..._band_00_y5-242.png`, 383 × 238 px, baseline y ≈ 223):

| Metric | Value | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| peaks above 8 px | 10 | well-resolved pattern |
| FWHM (8 px threshold) | 3–6 px | needle-like, near-vertical sides |
| baseline noise σ (non-peak columns) | **0.52 px** | no counting noise |
| tallest peak | 168 px | dynamic range ~2.5 decades |
| background | flat, no amorphous halo | no sample holder / air scatter |

The Raman panel in the same figure visibly contains high-frequency noise, and
its traces share no texture with the XRD bands (`noise_corr = 0.07` where
computable). Two bands return the fingerprint verdict
`noise_incomparable: flat white background(s) (vector re-render or lossless
re-plot)`, which independently supports the idealised-line reading rather than
a figure-wide JPEG artefact.

`image_dup_orb.py` on Fig. 1 finds 35 matches / 20 inliers between the XRD and
Raman panels (0.57). Visual inspection shows the match is driven by shared
axis/legend lettering, not by a copied curve: the two panels have different
peak positions. Recorded as benign.

Fig. 3 (LSV/Tafel) contains strong ORB pairs (95 matches / 70 inliers between
a and b; 130/59 within f4) because the same electrochemical series is plotted
at different x-ranges. These are legitimate re-plots, not copies.

## Interpretation

The XRD trace is drawn as an idealised line: peaks have almost vertical flanks,
the baseline has sub-pixel noise, and there is no amorphous background. That is
consistent with the notice's “features inconsistent with genuine experimental
XRD measurements”, and is the kind of anomaly a human auditor would flag by
eye. It is **not** a validated test of genuineness: vector-plotted, smoothed
XRD from a real instrument can look similar, and the actual raw counts are not
in the article.

**Grade: Level 2** — measurable anomaly, insufficient to conclude; raw data and
an XRD expert are required. The detecting step here is the geometric
measurement, not a database hit or a hash match.
